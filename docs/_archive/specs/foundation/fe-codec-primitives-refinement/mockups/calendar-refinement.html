What changed (every item is a token/variant lever, no new palette)

  • Month title → `$display` serif, medium weight, tightened tracking (was heavier/flatter).
  • Weekday row → eyebrow: uppercase, wide tracking, `$colorTextMuted/faint`, small (was a plain label).
  • Day numbers → `tabular-nums` so columns align; out-of-month in `faint`.
  • Today → accent number + a short accent underline (a "moment" mark) instead of a filled brand circle.
  • Weekend & out-of-month → subtle paper shift (`$colorSurfaceSecondary` family) for rhythm.
  • Chips → accent category dot + sans caption; +K more → eyebrow-muted.
  • Grid → hairline cells, generous min-height for air.
