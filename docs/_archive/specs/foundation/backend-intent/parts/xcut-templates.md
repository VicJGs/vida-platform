# Backend Intent — Part A: xcut-templates

> Cross-cutting source: **ADR-009** (`docs/core/decisions/ADR-009-templates-fork-on-instantiate.md`) —
> Templates are portable seed bundles instantiated by FORK (deep copy, no live link). Mechanical predicates:
> **capability-framework.md T1–T5 + A1** (`docs/specs/foundation/capability-framework.md:133-137,143,239,262`).
> Scope note: there is **no dedicated templates substrate spec** (`docs/core/templates/` does not exist) and
> **no service stub** (`packages/core/src/services/*templat*` absent); the `templates` STORE was already
> authored in the schema-foundation phase (`docs/roadmap/m1.0-closeout.md:7` — "`templates`" among the
> authored tables). Templates COMPOSE the sibling xcut tiers — Spaces (ADR-006), Views (ADR-007), Capability
> (ADR-008) — so a template row that seeds tags/views/enabled-blocks DELEGATES to those services; its own
> unique logic is capture + fork + the authored-vs-accumulated invariant + terminal states.
> DISAMBIGUATION: module-local "templates" (check-in `check_in_templates`, `session_templates`) are NOT the
> ADR-009 Spaces Template primitive and are owned by their own module Part A files — excluded here.
