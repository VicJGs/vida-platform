Past as written record. Mon–Wed are tinted like a filled page — an auto-composed "how it went" line + the day's completions (struck, muted) + focus logged. You scroll back and read your life, not empty cells.
2
Habit threads. Each habit is one continuous line across the week; kept days are filled nodes, misses are dotted gaps. The thread you don't want to break, made visible.
3
Open-time surfacing. Today shows a genuinely free block and offers to place your deep work / a study session / a habit into it — the calendar plans with you.
4
Purpose budget. Hours this week by the purpose each block serves, with a gentle flag on a value you've given none — time measured against what matters.
5
Drift flag. A must-do that keeps getting pushed shows "moved 3×" — honest about slippage, nudging a real decision instead of silently rotting.
