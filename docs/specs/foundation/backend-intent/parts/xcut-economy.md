# Backend Intent — Part A: xcut-economy

> **Context:** cross-cutting points-economy / consequence engine. Owning ADR: **ADR-012**. Composes the
> effect rail (ADR-004 §3.2.5 — inherited, NOT re-asserted), measurement's `Binding` (ADR-011 — a rule
> condition *is* a binding), and the ADR-010 sandbox (user-authored rules).
> **Substrate status:** the economy primitives are **BUILT** (AR-7 Increment 4, FINAL 2026-08-03) at
> `packages/core/src/foundation/economy/` — `currencies` + `consequence_ledger` (append-only, derived
> balances), migration 006, `computeBalance`, `grant_currency`/`lock_currency` handlers + rail wiring,
> `dispatchConsequence` coordinator, currency registry + XP seed. `[FACT: economy/CLAUDE.md; current-spec.md:5-25]`
> **Ownership rule (the core boundary — BR-8/§3.11):** the economy substrate OWNS `currencies` / the
> `consequence_ledger` / the 4 derived quantities / lock-not-destroy / the consequence coordinator / the
> effect-class assignment of the 4 typed effects. It does NOT own the write rail (ADR-004), conditions
> (ADR-011), the sandbox (ADR-010), money/`store` (Finance), the `rules`/achievements/level/clamp surface
> (MOD-gamification), `surface_notification` dispatch (MOD-notifications), or the `user_profile` split
> targets (Check-ins/Preferences). `[FACT: current-business-rules.md BR-8 L19; ADR-012 §3.11 L88-95]`
> **Peripheral observer, in-degree 1:** nothing core depends on the System; only Notifications reads it +
> Journals reads its `summary(date)` provider. No cycle. `[INTERP: ADR-012 §3.11 L95]`
> **Provisional homes** below name **economy-substrate (ADR-012, `@vida/core` foundation)** for the shared
> engine surfaces; downstream-owned items (money/rules/achievements/level/notifications/profile-split) are
> homed to their OWNING module with the economy composition noted. Final homing is P3.
