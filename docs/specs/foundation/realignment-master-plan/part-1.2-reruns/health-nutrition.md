## §1. Context — why this re-run, and the true gap shape

Health: Nutrition is the **fuel pillar** of the recovered Health domain (2 of 2, with Training) — authored Phase-2 from the 9-Nutrition-DB Notion intake, atop a Health domain that has **no v2 schema at all**. Its intended model is rich (Ingredient/Dish libraries · Nutritional Plan · Daily Meal Plans · Meals · Shopping · the food-side of the Core Adaptation engine) and its **boundaries are already crisp**: it composes Core's `health_goals` / `health_metrics` / Adaptation engine (not its own), Finance for cost, Spaces for tags/blocks, Knowledge for dishes-as-a-kind. It is **explicitly not a session** (a meal is scheduled/logged, HN-2) and **not the goal/metric owner** (HN-4).

**So nutrition is the near-twin of fiscal-legal** — another net-new, pure-gap unit riding the same X*/AI* families — with **one added driver (006, tags)** and therefore **one forward-token reframe** on top of the pure adds. The gap the sweep names is exact: (a) AI-generated rows lack `source=ai` (AI6); (b) the food-DB / barcode / photo ingestion is described as "opt-in extension-model" but **names no X*/AI* token**; (c) tagging is described ("Spaces universal tags", HN-12) but the entity model declares an **inline `meals.tags` column** instead of `entity_tags` (SP1). The framework names nutrition **verbatim** three times — **X3 "Health food-DB"**, **X11 "Health photo"**, **AI6 "Health"** — so membership is airtight.
