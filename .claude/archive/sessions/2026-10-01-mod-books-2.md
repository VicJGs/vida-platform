## Decisions Made
- **setChapters = upsert-preserve-tracking, NOT replace-all** — it is the only TOC-edit path; a replace would wipe
  reading progress on a title fix (violates BK-17 anti-lost). UNIQUE key = the stable identity.
- **UNIQUE = app-layer in-array pre-check + DB backstop** — the repo surfaces no typed unique error; no-FK pattern.
- **ISBN grant identity = Books-owned `BOOKS_ISBN_INTEGRATION_ID`, inert at M1** — naming Books' own integration is
  legit (entity_type/sub-type precedent); synthesizing a downstream grant-map = No-Guessing. Fetch/normalization = M2.
- **setChapters is per-row (NOT atomic)** — documented eventually-consistent/re-drivable; the Repository has no
  `updateInTx`/`deleteInTx`, and a declarative set is idempotent. Inventing an atomicity mandate = over-correction.
- **Chapter READ path = carry-forward** — not in the frozen S2 seam-contract; home where `BookView` expands.
